The whole workspace

Thirteen destinations.
One login, one memory.

Focus Flow is not a collection of disconnected widgets. Your tasks, sessions, decks, maps and communities all live in the same account and feed the same progress stats — so what you do in the timer shows up in your streak, and what KIRO generates lands straight in your decks.

  1. 01Home

    Your day at a glance: active tasks, focus session, flow goals, activity stream and the consistency matrix.

  2. 02Timer

    A distraction-free focus session with play / reset controls, a session counter and an audio cue when time is up.

  3. 03Monthly Goals

    Pick the big things that matter this month, track active vs. completed, and keep the big picture in view.

  4. 04Progress

    Streaks, weekly vs. monthly pace, a 30-day and full-year consistency graph and a breakdown of your milestones.

  5. 05Community

    Create or join study spaces, post in channels, run announcements, and moderate with roles, join requests and bans.

  6. 06KIRO

    The AI assistant: web research with citations, video summaries, notes, flashcards and study plans.

  7. 07Planner

    Plan in day, week, month or year view, then step backwards and forwards through time without losing context.

  8. 08Calendar

    Month grid with events you can create and edit in place — deadlines sit next to everything else.

  9. 09ToDo

    Daily task lists with completion counts, weekly pace and a daily goal so you know when you are actually done.

  10. 10Notes

    Write it down, then let KIRO turn it into a summary, a detailed study note or a concept breakdown.

  11. 11Flash Cards

    Build decks manually or generate them from a prompt, study them with a flip player, and read your recall analytics.

  12. 12Mind Maps

    Branch a topic into a visual tree, auto-arrange it, colour the branches and elaborate on any node with AI.

  13. 13Settings

    Profile, avatar, theme and your own AI keys (OpenRouter, Google Gemini or a custom endpoint), saved and masked.

01 — Focus engine

A timer that is actually part of your day.

Most timers stop at counting down. Focus Flow's session sits inside your dashboard: the tasks you are working through are on the left, the activity you have already banked is on the right, and the time you just spent lands in your consistency matrix the moment you finish.

  • Focus session, front and centre. A huge legible clock, play and reset, and a session counter that keeps you honest.
  • Audio cues included. Notification and beep sounds so you can look away from the screen without losing the session.
  • Active tasks beside it. See today's list without leaving the timer — check one off and keep going.
  • Flow goals with real progress. "Achieve 2h daily focus" fills up from your actual sessions, not from wishful checkboxes.
  • Monthly goals above the daily grind. Set the big targets once a month and watch them convert into daily work.

02 — Meet KIRO

The assistant that shows its work.

KIRO is wired into every part of Focus Flow. Ask it anything and it streams back an answer with the steps it took on screen — searching the web, pulling a video transcript, or writing your deck. You can watch it think, and check every source it used.

🔎 Searching the web for sources…
📄 Found 6 results, deduplicated and cleaned
🧠 Drafting answer with citations…
💬 Streaming response
Every source is a link that opens in a new tab — nothing is paraphrased away.

Live web research

For anything that moves fast — news, versions, deadlines — KIRO runs a real search, pulls the snippets, and answers with clickable citations inline.

YouTube video analysis

Drop a video link and it extracts the transcript, plays the video in the chat, and hands back a structured summary of what was actually said.

Three study modes

Ask for a Summary, a Detailed Study Note with definitions, examples and practice questions, or a Concept Breakdown of how ideas relate.

Writes straight into your workspace

“Generate 10 flashcards about organic chemistry” creates a real deck in Flash Cards. Notes turn into summaries. No copy-paste between tools.

Math and markdown, rendered

Answers come back with working LaTeX and clean markdown, so derivations and code blocks stay readable instead of collapsing into $$ soup.

Your keys, your models

Bring your own OpenRouter, Google Gemini or custom endpoint key in Settings. Keys are masked, saved to your account and stay in your control.

  • Colour branches so each stream of a topic is visually separate at a glance.

  • Planner in day, week, month and year views, with events you can create and edit in place.
  • Calendar grid for the month, so deadlines never live in a different app.
  • ToDo with completion counts, weekly pace and a daily goal target.
  • Notes with AI summarisation, study notes and concept breakdowns.
